01 About

About me

I am an Argentinian-Italian Compositing Artist based in Mendoza with 11+ years of experience in VFX compositing, working with skilled professionals in feature films, TV commercials, cinematic video games and TV shows.

At work, I always look for the most effective way to achieve objectives with the available tools, and I enjoy collaborating and helping other team members when needed. I am currently looking for new professional challenges where I can develop my skills together with talented teams doing amazing VFX shots.

I love travelling, cooking and movies.
